# ICAO Annexes & Regulatory Framework

The Convention on International Civil Aviation (the Chicago Convention, 1944) establishes the principles and technical basis for international air navigation. The technical specifications are codified in **19 Annexes**, containing Standards and Recommended Practices (SARPs).

---

## 2. Deep Dive: Core Operational Annexes

### Annex 6 — Operation of Aircraft
- **Part I — International Commercial Air Transport (Aeroplanes)**:
  - Minimum Equipment List (MEL) and Configuration Deviation List (CDL) requirements.
  - Flight Data Recorder (FDR) and Cockpit Voice Recorder (CVR) carriage standards.
  - Extended Diversion Time Operations (EDTO) / ETOPS criteria.
  - Fatigue Risk Management Systems (FRMS) and maximum Flight Duty Period (FDP) rules.
- **Part III — International Operations (Helicopters)**:
  - Performance Class 1, 2, and 3 operations in hostile vs. non-hostile environments.
  - Heliport operating minima and single-engine failure contingency procedures.

### Annex 13 — Aircraft Accident and Incident Investigation
- **Sole Objective**: "The sole objective of the investigation of an accident or incident shall be the prevention of accidents and incidents. It is not the purpose of this activity to apportion blame or liability."
- **Mandatory Reporting**: Thresholds distinguishing *Accidents*, *Serious Incidents*, and *Incidents*.
- **ADREP Integration**: Obligation of the State of Occurrence to submit preliminary and formal ADREP data reports to ICAO.

### Annex 19 — Safety Management
- Mandates the implementation of:
  1. **State Safety Programme (SSP)**: Established by the State civil aviation authority.
  2. **Safety Management System (SMS)**: Implemented by certified operators, maintenance organizations, aerodromes, and ATS providers.
  3. **Safety Performance Indicators (SPI)** and **Acceptable Level of Safety Performance (ALoSP)**.
  4. Non-punitive safety reporting culture (Just Culture framework).

## 3. Domestic Harmonization: CAAN Regulatory Alignment

Under the Civil Aviation Authority of Nepal (CAAN) Act:

- **NCAR (Nepalese Civil Airworthiness Requirements)**: Transposes Annex 8 technical airworthiness directives, continuing airworthiness management (CAMO), and Part-145 maintenance approvals.
- **FOR (Flight Operations Requirements - Aeroplane / Helicopter)**: Enforces Annex 6 operational compliance, crew complement rules, mountain terrain clearance margins, and monsoon SOPs.
- **PELR (Personnel Licensing Requirements)**: Implements Annex 1 requirements for ATPL, CPL, PPL, AME, and ATCO licensing and Class 1/2 medical renewals.
- **CAR-14 (Civil Aviation Requirements - Aerodromes)**: Transposes Annex 14 infrastructure specifications for STOL airfields and international airports (Tribhuvan VNKT, Gautam Buddha VNPW, Pokhara VNPK).
